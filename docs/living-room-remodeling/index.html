Living rooms

What to expect

Your living room is where family and friends come together, which makes it one of the most important spaces in your home. Yoerger Renovations helps homeowners create comfortable, inviting and functional living spaces that reflect their style and meet their everyday needs.

Update the flooring, install custom trim, add built-in shelving, improve the lighting, open up the layout or transform the whole room. Whatever the scope, Ryan brings quality craftsmanship and attention to detail every step of the way, so the space feels welcoming, adds value and becomes the place to relax and make memories.

Good questions

Before you call

Can you open up a wall between rooms?
Often, yes. Ryan checks whether the wall is load-bearing and explains what it takes either way before you decide.
Will new floors match the rest of the house?
That's the goal. Ryan helps you pick a plank or hardwood-look floor that ties into adjoining rooms, and can carry it through for a seamless run.
How disruptive is a living room refresh?
Less than you'd think. Flooring, trim and paint go quickly, and Ryan keeps the job site clean and tells you what's happening each day.
